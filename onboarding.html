Step 6 of 7 — conditional offer

Pre-hire authorizations

You have a written conditional offer. Before your start date we need your signed authorization for two things: a background check, and — if your role drives — a check of your driving record. Read each one; they are short and they are separate on purpose.

We are not asking for your Social Security number or date of birth on this page, and you should not type them here. Once you sign below, the consumer reporting agency will email you a link to their secure portal, where you enter those identifiers directly to them. We never store them. Your Form I-9 documents are shown in person on your first day — not uploaded, not photographed, not emailed.

A. Background check

The box below is the disclosure required by the federal Fair Credit Reporting Act. It contains the disclosure and nothing else — no waiver, no release, no other terms. Your authorization comes after it, separately.

DISCLOSURE REGARDING BACKGROUND INVESTIGATION

Gulf ProClean may obtain information about you from a consumer reporting agency for employment purposes. Accordingly, you may be the subject of a "consumer report" and/or an "investigative consumer report" which may include information about your character, general reputation, personal characteristics, and mode of living.

These reports may contain information regarding your criminal history, social security verification, motor vehicle records, and verification of your education or employment history.

You have the right, upon written request made within a reasonable time, to request whether a consumer report has been run about you, and to request a copy of your report. These searches will be conducted by a consumer reporting agency, whose name, address and telephone number are provided to you with the secure link that follows this authorization.

The scope of this disclosure is all-encompassing, allowing Gulf ProClean to obtain from any outside organization all manner of consumer reports throughout the course of your employment to the extent permitted by law.

B. Driving record

Your role involves driving to job sites, so we also check your motor vehicle record. This is a separate authorization from the one above, as contemplated by section 768.096(1)(d), Florida Statutes.

C. Authorization to work in the United States

Nothing to upload — this is an acknowledgment of what happens on your first day.

  • You complete Section 1 of Form I-9 no later than your first day of work.
  • You present original documents establishing identity and work authorization by your third business day.
  • You choose which documents to present from the Form I-9 Lists of Acceptable Documents, which are attached to your offer email. We will not ask for a specific document, will not ask for more documents than the law requires, and will not reject a document that reasonably appears genuine.
  • Gulf ProClean runs each new hire through E-Verify within three business days after the first day worked, consistent with section 448.095, Florida Statutes. It is never run before hire.
  • If E-Verify returns a Tentative Nonconfirmation, you receive the written referral notice and information on contesting it. Your employment, hours, pay and start date are not affected while you resolve it.

D. Drug-free workplace

Gulf ProClean maintains a drug-free workplace under sections 440.101–440.102, Florida Statutes. The full written policy — substances tested, the licensed laboratory used, medications that can affect results, consequences of refusal, and employee assistance resources — is attached to your offer email.

E. Signature

Sign and date

A copy of everything you signed is emailed to you immediately. If we later decide not to move forward because of something in the report, you will first receive a copy of the report and a Summary of Your Rights, and at least five business days to respond — see our hiring process page.

Signed

Most checks come back in two to five business days. We will confirm your start date as soon as it clears.
