Privacy Policy

Effective September 9, 2026. This policy covers the Gulf ProClean website, the Gulf ProClean customer app, and the Gulf ProClean Crew employee app (together, the "Services"), operated by Gulf ProClean ("we", "us").

What we collect

  • Account details — your email address and a password (stored only as a salted hash, never in plain text).
  • Booking details — your name, phone number, service address, billing name and address, the services you choose, scheduling preferences and any notes you add for our crew.
  • Payment — payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number; we keep only the Stripe payment reference and the amount paid.
  • Messages you send us — through the contact form, refund or plan-change requests.
  • Job applications and vendor submissions — only what you enter in those forms.
  • Site usage — anonymous page-view counts per page. No IP addresses, cookies or visitor identity are stored for analytics.

Crew app (employees only): we record the checklist items you complete, clock-in and clock-out times, job notes, sign-off names, and start-of-shift supply checks. The Crew app does not track your location.

How we use it

  • To schedule, perform, confirm and remind you about cleaning visits, and to process payments and refunds.
  • To assign the right technician to each visit and to keep our crews stocked and on time.
  • To respond to your messages and requests, and to consider job applications and vendor bids.
  • To keep the Services secure and working.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Stripe (payments), Cloudflare (hosting), Neon (database hosting) and Google (transactional email) — each only to provide their service to us.
  • Our technicians see the name, address, phone number and service notes for the visits assigned to them, and nothing else about you.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

Cookies

We use one strictly necessary, secure session cookie to keep you signed in. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

Booking and payment records are kept for as long as needed for accounting and tax purposes. You can delete your account at any time (see below); we then remove your login and contact details and keep only the anonymized booking history our records require.

Your choices

  • Delete your account from the app or at delete-account.html.
  • Update your contact and address details from your account page.
  • Email us at gulfproclean@gmail.com to request a copy of your data or ask a question.

Children

The Services are for adults arranging cleaning services and for our employees. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, notify you by email.
