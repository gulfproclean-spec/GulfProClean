Criminal history

Our technicians work inside occupied homes and hold keys and alarm codes for businesses, so Florida law (section 768.096, Florida Statutes) expects us to ask this. Please read how we use the answer before you give it.

A conviction does not automatically disqualify you. We look at what the offense was, how long ago it happened, what you have done since, and whether it actually relates to the job you are applying for. Answer honestly — being untruthful here is what ends an application, not the record itself.

Do not disclose arrests that did not lead to conviction, charges that were dismissed, adjudication withheld without conviction, sealed or expunged records, or juvenile matters. We are not asking about those and you are not required to tell us.
Notices
Drug-free workplace. Gulf ProClean maintains a drug-free workplace. Positions involving driving, operating powered equipment, or unsupervised access to customer property are subject to drug testing, including pre-employment testing after a conditional offer, and testing on reasonable suspicion or after a workplace accident, in accordance with sections 440.101–440.102, Florida Statutes. You will receive the full written policy, including the list of substances tested and your right to contest a result with the Medical Review Officer within five working days, before any test is administered.
Employment eligibility. All new hires complete federal Form I-9 on their first day of work. Where required, Gulf ProClean also verifies employment eligibility through E-Verify within three business days of the first day worked, as required by section 448.095, Florida Statutes. E-Verify is not used to pre-screen applicants and is never run before a hire.
