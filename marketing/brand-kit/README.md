# Gulf ProClean — Brand Kit (Step 1)

Everything needed to look the same everywhere: on the website, social profiles,
shirts, the car and every piece of paper. Using the same look, words and colors
every time is what makes people recognize you, and people trust businesses they
recognize.

`logo/png/` has the same logos as transparent PNGs, 2400 px wide, for Canva, Word and
social apps. `logo/logo-sheet.png` shows them all side by side.

**Rules**
- Leave clear space around the logo at least the height of the "G" on all sides.
- Don't stretch it, recolor it, add shadows or outlines, or put it on a busy photo.
- Don't use the horizontal logo smaller than 1.25 in. (print) or 150 px (screen).

## 3. Colors

| Name | HEX | RGB | Use for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Navy** | `#153238` | 21, 50, 56 | Main color: backgrounds, headlines, uniforms |
| **Gold** | `#B68235` | 182, 130, 53 | Accent: the anchor, "ProClean", buttons, highlights |
| **Dark Gold** | `#8A6221` | 138, 98, 33 | Gold text on light backgrounds (easier to read) |
| **Cream** | `#F5F2EC` | 245, 242, 236 | Soft backgrounds; "Gulf" on navy |
| **Slate** | `#3D4A4D` | 61, 74, 77 | Body text |

Rough guide: about 60% navy or cream, 30% white, 10% gold. Gold is the accent, not the main color.

**For printers and apparel shops:** ask them to match these HEX values. If they need a
Pantone number, ask for their closest match to `#153238` (a deep teal-navy) and `#B68235`
(an antique gold), and **get a printed proof before paying for a large run.**

## 5. How we sound

- **Friendly, direct and local.** Talk like a neighbor, not a corporation: "We just cleaned a home on your street."
- **Confident, never pushy.** No ALL-CAPS shouting, no "BEST IN TOWN!!!"
- **Only say what's true.** Don't advertise "licensed," "insured," "bonded" or a guarantee until the
  paperwork is in hand. These files deliberately leave those claims out.
- Always end with one clear next step: call, scan, or visit gulfproclean.com.

## 6. Print files, ready to order

All PDFs in `print/` include a **0.125 in. bleed** (the color runs past the cut line so
there are no white edges) and keep text inside the safe zone. Upload them as-is to any printer
(Vistaprint, GotPrint, UPrinting, or a local shop) and choose "my file includes bleed."
Previews are in `print/preview/`; the editable HTML sources are in `print/source/`.

| File | Finished size | Order this | Approx. cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| `business-card-3.5x2.pdf` (2 pages: front, back) | 3.5 × 2 in. | 500 cards, 16 pt matte, double-sided | $25–60 |
| `door-hanger-4.25x11.pdf` (2 pages) | 4.25 × 11 in. | 500, 14 pt, **standard hanger die-cut (hole + slit)** | $90–170 |
| `flyer-residential-8.5x11.pdf` | 8.5 × 11 in. | 250, 100 lb gloss text | $40–90 |
| `flyer-commercial-8.5x11.pdf` | 8.5 × 11 in. | 100, 100 lb gloss text (for walk-ins) | $25–60 |
| `car-magnet-24x12.pdf` | 24 × 12 in. | **2** (one per door), 30 mil, rounded corners | $60–120 for the pair |

Notes
- **Door hanger:** the top 2.4 in. is left plain so the printer's hole-and-slit die-cut won't cut
  through anything. If the printer's template puts the hole somewhere else, send me their template and I'll adjust.
  The crew writes the street name on the blank line by hand. That's what makes "your neighbor" feel real.
- **Business card back:** the "Referred by: ____" line is there on purpose. Write the customer's name on
  cards you hand them, so you know who sent each new client when the referral program starts.
- **Car magnets:** put them on flat door panels only (not over body lines). Take them off once a week to
  wipe underneath, and before car washes, so they don't damage the paint.
- **Offer wording** ("First-time customers save 10% on any plan*") matches what the website advertises
  today. If that offer changes, update the text before reprinting.
