### Account security (do this, it protects the business)
- Create every account with **gulfproclean@gmail.com**, not a personal email, so the business owns them.
- Turn on **2-step verification** on that Gmail first (Google Account → Security), using an
  authenticator app (Google Authenticator) rather than text messages if you can.

  (Google Password Manager is free and built in).
- Save each platform's **backup codes** somewhere offline.
- Never give anyone your password. Add helpers later as **page roles/managers**, so you can remove them anytime.
- Watch for scam DMs saying "your page will be deleted, click here." Meta, Google and TikTok don't contact you that way.

**Settings to change** (Settings and privacy):
- Security → **2-step verification** on.
- Privacy → keep the account **public** (business accounts need to be).
- Privacy → Comments → turn on **Filter spam and offensive comments**.
- Privacy → **Suggest your account to others → Contacts and Facebook friends on.** Your
  first viewers will be people who already know you, and they're the most likely to share.
- Business tools → turn on **Business Suite / analytics** so you can see which videos bring calls later.

**Why TikTok matters here:** TikTok shows videos to people *near you* and to people who don't follow
you yet. A brand-new account with zero followers can still reach local viewers, which is
rare on other platforms. Cleaning before/after videos are a popular, highly shareable category.

### Launch video (post this the day the account is created)
A 20–30 second, vertical, filmed-on-your-phone introduction. People hire faces they recognize,
so you on camera does more than any logo.

| Seconds | Show | Say (or on-screen text) |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 | You in your Gulf ProClean shirt, close to camera | **Text on screen:** "Veteran-owned cleaning on the Emerald Coast ⚓" |
| 2–10 | You talking to camera (car with magnet behind you if you have it) | "Hi, I'm [first name]. I served [years] in the [branch], and now my family and I run Gulf ProClean." |
| 10–20 | Quick clips: wiping a counter, shining a faucet, vacuum lines | "We clean homes, vacation rentals and businesses from Pensacola to Panama City Beach." |
| 20–28 | Back to you, smiling | "Follow along for before-and-afters and cleaning tips, and if you need a clean, the link's in our bio." |

**Caption:**
```
Meet Gulf ProClean ⚓ Veteran-owned, family-operated cleaning from Pensacola to Panama City Beach. Follow for before & afters + cleaning tips! #pensacola #destin #30a #emeraldcoast #cleaningtiktok
```
**Filming tips:** good daylight (face a window), phone at eye level on a tripod, use TikTok's
auto-captions (tap **Captions** before posting) since many people watch without sound.
Use only TikTok's **Commercial Music Library** for background music. Business accounts
aren't licensed to use regular trending songs.

After posting: **pin** the launch video to the top of your profile (tap ••• on the video → Pin),
and share it to your Facebook and Instagram stories.

### Next two TikToks (week 1–2, before you have customers)
1. **"What's in a professional cleaner's kit"**: lay out your supplies, 15 seconds, name each item.
2. **A real before/after** from a friend's or family member's home (with their permission). Oven,
   shower glass or stovetop work best. Film the "before," then the "after," same angle.

The full 10-video list and posting schedule are in the acquisition plan (Step 8).
